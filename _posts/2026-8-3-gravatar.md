holy shit I just opened a time capsule.

<!--more-->

Gravatar. The "all in one" avatar platform. You change your profile on there, it changes everywhere. I had COMPLETELY forgotten about this. A little while ago I made a new account for...something, server hosting stuff I think. It pulled my good ol' fanmade Phantom Bonnie avatar from github, which yknow, odd, but maybe it grabbed it from GitHub. Today, I was making a funny minecraft resource pack changing the end text to a video transcript my friends and I did (as one does), and checking it off of my new Vikunja board, noticed I could change my avatar to Gravatar. Clicked it, hey, there it is again! GitHub related maybe? I looked it up and signed in...

[Holy shit.](https://gravatar.com/phantomenderium)

I'm immediately greeted with my description - "Le Dab". I though dab humor was cringe back in 2016, which was ironic, considering-2016?! And right below it, was 2 links. 1, to tomgames, my spinoff of scottgames back in the day (I totally wasn't a fnaf fanboy), which was at the time my testing of messing with HTML after taking an online course, and this was hosted through azure sites with help from my dads job because he was able to get a domain with it. Stopped working long ago, but look at me now with my own domains and self hosting! The other one was a wordpress blog for "Five Nights in a Basement", a video featuring me and some friends where we made a trailer for a FNAF spinoff...in my basement...made the whole thing with the Apple movie editor on my iPod touch. According to the blog, we were to make a movie. Current progress: 0% done! Yeah 13 year old me, that's still true buddy, sorry.

wait I have a wordpress blog?

[I do](https://fniab.wordpress.com/)! An entire blog focused on this video...one single post, which noted the trailer. That's it. No idea what our plans were but we never did anything besides that one video. Wow. Seriously, talk about a blast from the past.

Gravatar is certainly a pretty nice platform looking at it. I think I made it FOR the wordpress, and then later when I made the GitHub account it came over. I had one other avatar saved that I can freely swap too - a pixel art I made of Withered Bonnie's face. Dude, I could add so many avatars to this. And it has an API I could mess around with?! Totally will be looking into this more.

Internet time capsules are something else.
