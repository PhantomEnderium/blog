Shooters have always been a favorite genre of mine. Right now it's been kinda a toss up between Deadlock, Marvel Rivals, Overwatch, and Fortnite (FUCK TIM SWEENEY). But I probably wouldn't be into them as much without the original one that got me into the genre: Team Fortress 2.
<!--more-->

TF2 was a game I discovered on Steam not long after playing through Portal (which was my first steam game and one of the first computer games I've played!). I don't recall exactly what exactly got me into it, maybe it was the SFM videos people made back in the day. Regardless I sunk a TON of hours into it. I remember trying to convince my dad to let me buy a 50 cent Crit-a-Cola just so I could get premium on my account (and getting him pissed off over the ringing of the default hit noise he could hear from across the house). Buying a Team Spirit paint for the given Mann Co. cap to style all the classes. Getting into the basics of trading. New updates bringing fun weapons. Hours spent running around as Scout in Casual or goofing off in Community servers. Those were good times.

Then I got scammed.

To be specific, I hadn't played TF2 in a couple years at this point, I got a discord message with someone saying they and some friends thought I "was someone else" and they "mass reported me". Ended up talking to some other guy who said my items needed to be verified. Me, trying to be helpful, went along with it. In hindsight, I've never felt more stupid for falling for it, it was EXTREMELY obvious, and the thing I fell for hardest was the fake ban button on my steam account, which was a simple HTML edit, I of all people should've known that. I lost basically all my stranges, cosmetics, and taunts. I never had any unusuals, looking back on backpack.tf I had maybe $20 worth of items total, but it was still a hard blow, even though I haven't played in a while, since it was stuff I built up over the years. My account being so much more bare was just, disheartening. I tried to get back into the game later on, but with that (and the blatant amount of cheaters at the time), it just wasn't worth it.

Cut to a few weeks ago. Bored on my own one night, Nicole is passed out in bed from working the late hours she does. I decided, bored enough, why not? I launch up the game, look through my inventory a bit, and go back to good ol' scrap.tf, an automated trading site I've used for years and years along with playing. Figured if I do want to get back into the game, worse case I needed to spend no more than $100 for everything back? I severly underestimated how little I lost currency wise.

Scrap.tf immediaetly shows me something looking through my remaining inventory. Most things I have are worth a few scrap, only weapons and some crates. But I had a few old End of the Line crates.

20\. Refined. Each.

I had a couple others from earlier Christmas' that were also a couple refined. Holy shit, I was sitting on free currency. Not a LOT compared to multi key unusuals, but I traded them all to a bot and got 2 keys and some extra refined. I could buy maybe a few hats to make up what I lost. Apparently though, normal craft hats are CHEAP. Like less than 2 refined. I was able to secure all of the old cosmetics I had back, some extra normal weapons since I was missing ones that were originally strange, I had them back! I traded one of the keys for refined, and while it's pretty much a net negative, I opened a crate with the other key, and ended up with a Pretty Boy's Pocket Pistol Strangifier, which that weapon was one of the stranges I had lost! I applied it to a new one I picked up and went from there.

Over the next couple of days, messed around playing again, buying more stuff, including getting back a Strange Baby Face's Blaster that I also used to have, and applied a nice Mandarian Sheen Killstreak Kit to it as well. Most recently, bought about 10 keys off of marketplace.tf to trade for refinded to buy taunts for cheap, as vs the official store were about 1/4th the price, so I had all my old taunts back plus more. I even got one of them unusual, as apparently unusual taunts are not much more priced vs regular, which is interesting considering unusuals are supposed to be rare. My account is now worth more than it ever was before.

While it's not the same ones I originally had, it brought me back to a better position than I was before. Feels weird having stuff again, and enjoying this old yet memorable game again. Will I get lucky to finally unbox an unusual cosmetic? Probably not, and technically it's better to spend the money on currency to trade than gambling and most likely rolling a net negative (1 key into a 5 refined strange? Not super worth it). But regardless, feels great to be back into it. Now to just get Nicole and our friends into the game.

Maybe I'll finally get the Australium Force-a-Nature I've been hoping to luck out on.
